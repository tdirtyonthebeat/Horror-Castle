# Horror Castle v0.12.3 — Ritual Separation

The first independent-core listening test showed BIND, SACRIFICE and SUMMON
were too similar, especially when CRYPT and TOWER were configured similarly.

## New identities

### BIND — Harmonic Fusion
BIND keeps both chamber identities but creates sign-preserving cross-harmonics.
It should read as thicker, fused and glued rather than simply crossfaded.

- Depth: strength of the bond/cross-harmonics
- Fury: saturation / heat
- Feedback: coherence push
- Mix: dry/wet Ritual amount
- Width: stereo width

### SACRIFICE — Balanced Ring Destruction
SACRIFICE suppresses much of the original fundamental and emphasizes
multiplicative sidebands.

- Depth: how completely the fundamental is sacrificed
- Fury: ring/metallic intensity
- Feedback: adds CRYPT/TOWER difference energy
- Mix: dry/wet Ritual amount
- Width: stereo width

### SUMMON — Tuned Regenerative Resonator
SUMMON now excites a bounded two-pole resonator instead of simply smoothing
the dry signal through a feedback state.

- Depth: resonant pitch (~72 Hz to ~2.3 kHz)
- Fury: excitation strength
- Feedback: resonance/persistence
- Mix: dry/wet Ritual amount
- Width: stereo width

POSSESS and DEVOUR are unchanged.
