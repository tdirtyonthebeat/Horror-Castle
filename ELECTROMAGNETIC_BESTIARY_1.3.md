# Horror Castle v1.3 — Electromagnetic Bestiary

## Chamber IX research prototypes

These engines are compiled and regression-tested but are not yet exposed as a public generator index. Promotion remains append-only after CI and listening validation.

### CRYPT — POLTERGEIST
A charged-plate acoustic object.

- Four resonant plates accumulate alternating charge.
- Neighboring charge creates electrostatic attraction/repulsion that bends each plate's resonance.
- Charge leaks according to DREAD.
- Large potential differences cross a moving breakdown threshold and create bounded arc impulses.
- Expression changes the charging regime itself rather than acting only as output gain.

Intended public control mapping:
- SHAPE -> CHARGE
- CRYPT CHARACTER / DREAD -> leakage, stress, breakdown threshold
- velocity / mod wheel / pressure -> charging rate and arc energy

### TOWER — AURORA
A smooth charged-ring field.

- Five irrationally related resonant rings carry slowly moving charge.
- Neighboring and shared charge bend frequency/phase relationships.
- AETHER changes charge redistribution speed and shared-field motion.
- Unlike POLTERGEIST, AURORA has no hard discharge event; its energy moves continuously through the field.
- Expression changes how strongly the rings participate in the field.

Intended public control mapping:
- SHAPE -> FIELD STRENGTH
- TOWER CHARACTER / AETHER -> field motion and charge redistribution
- velocity / mod wheel / pressure -> field participation

## Promotion rule

MIRROR / ABYSS remain Chamber VIII prototypes until their gate is green. POLTERGEIST / AURORA remain Chamber IX prototypes until the electromagnetic numerical gate, full macOS plugin build, and listening tests all pass.

Existing generator indices are never renumbered.
