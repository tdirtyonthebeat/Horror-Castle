# v0.13 Signature Patch Recipes

These are listening recipes, not a formal preset bank yet.

## 1. Stone Below
CRYPT dominant. DREAD high, VA + Resonator, low filters, BIND lightly engaged, dark GRAVE.
Target: heavy unstable foundation with movement that feels structural rather than LFO-like.

## 2. Glass Spire
TOWER dominant. AETHER high, Wavetable + FM, higher filters, small POSSESS amount, medium GRAVE.
Target: bright supernatural upper structure without becoming a generic supersaw.

## 3. Blood Architecture
Balanced chambers. BLOOD Curse -> Filter Drive, HAUNT -> Tower Cutoff, BIND medium.
Target: velocity/envelope motion that makes the castle seem to breathe.

## 4. Bell Sacrifice
CRYPT Resonator + TOWER FM, SACRIFICE 70-90% wet, moderate Fury.
Target: metallic sum/difference spectra with little resemblance to either dry chamber.

## 5. Entity in the Stairwell
TOWER low, CRYPT medium, SUMMON with high Feedback and a moving Depth setting, long dark GRAVE.
Target: a tuned presence that appears behind the played note.
